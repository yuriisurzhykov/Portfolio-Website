Hero Graph Playground — WebGL Lens

Real Snell's-law refraction (refract()), not noise — each node refracts twice (entering and exiting the sphere), which is what actually inverts the image, not a single bend. The rim "highlight" isn't a painted-on white blob either: it's a Fresnel split (Schlick's approximation) between a reflected and a refracted sample of the same scene texture — reflecting the one warm patch in the corner of the grid below, the only actual light source in this scene. Drag a node over a line intersection to see it bend for real.
