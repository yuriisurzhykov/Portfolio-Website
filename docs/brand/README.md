# Brand

Personal identity explorations for Yurii Surzhykov.

- [`v3/`](v3/README.md), **Seal** (current): Y and S in the quarters of a gold
  Latin cross inside a square seal; S·D·G hallmark.
- [`v2/`](v2/README.md), **Billet**: a monolithic block cut by a Y-channel and
  two S-slots, deep Abyss ground, gold accent.
- [`v1/`](v1/README.md), **One line**: Y and S drawn as one continuous stroke
  on a light paper ground.
